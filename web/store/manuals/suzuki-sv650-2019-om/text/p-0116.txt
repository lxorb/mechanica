HEADLIGHT BEAM ADJUSTMENT
The headlight beam can be adjusted
both up and down or right and left if
necessary.
To adjust the beam up and down:
Loosen the adjuster 1. To adjust the
beam, move the headlight assembly
forward or backward.
To adjust the beam right and left:
Turn the adjuster 2 clockwise or
counterclockwise.
TURN SIGNAL LIGHT
To replace the turn signal light bulb,
follow these directions.
1. Remove the screw and take off
the lens.
2. Push in on the bulb, turn it to the
left, and pull it out.
3. To fit the replacement bulb, push it
in and twist it to the right while
pushing.
NOTICE
Overtightening the screws when
reinstalling the lens may cause
the lens to crack.
Tighten the screws only until they
are snug.
