The crossed-out wheeled bin symbol
A located on the battery label indi-
cates that a used battery should be
collected separately from ordinary
household waste.
The chemical symbol of “Pb” B indi-
cates the battery contains more than
0.004% lead.
By ensuring the used battery is dis-
posed of or recycled correctly, you will
help prevent potential negative conse-
quences for the environment and
human health, which could otherwise
be caused by inappropriate waste
handling of the battery. The recycling
of materials will help to conserve nat-
ural resources. For more detailed
information about disposing or recy-
cling of the used battery, consult your
Suzuki dealer.
