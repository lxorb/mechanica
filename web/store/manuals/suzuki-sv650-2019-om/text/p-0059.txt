RIDING TIPS
STARTING THE ENGINE
Before attempting to start the engine,
make sure:
•
The transmission is in neutral.
•
The engine stop switch is in the
“” position.
NOTE: This motorcycle is equipped
with an interlock system for the igni-
tion circuit and the starter circuit.
The engine can only be started if:
•
The transmission is in neutral, or
•
The transmission is in gear, the
side stand is fully up and the
clutch is disengaged.
NOTE: The fuel supply system stops
the engine when the motorcycle is
overturned. Turn off the ignition switch
before restarting the engine.
NOTICE
If the neutral indicator light and
the gear position indicator are not
giving proper indications, starting
the engine can cause serious
engine damage.
Before starting the engine, make
sure of the followings:
• When the neutral indicator light
comes on, the gear position
indicator should indicate “N”
(Neutral).
• When the neutral indicator light
goes off, the gear position indi-
cator should indicate either “1”,
“2”, “3”, “4”, “5” or “6”.
• If the neutral indicator light and
the gear position indicator are
not working properly, consult
your Suzuki dealer.
