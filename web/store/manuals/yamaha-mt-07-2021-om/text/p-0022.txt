Multi-function meter unit
The multi-function meter unit is also
equipped with a brightness control
mode.
WARNING
EWA12423
Be sure to stop the vehicle before
making any setting changes to the
multi-function meter unit. Changing
settings while riding can distract the
operator and increase the risk of an
accident.
EAU92550
TIP
The multi-function meter functions are
controlled using the “TRIP/INFO”
switch. 4-10 for more information.
EAU92191
Switching the display units
The display units can be switched be-
tween kilometers and miles.
To switch the display units
1. Set the multi-function display to
the odometer, and then turn the
vehicle off.
2. While pushing the “TRIP” switch,
turn the vehicle on.
3. Continue to push the “TRIP”
switch until the display units
change.
