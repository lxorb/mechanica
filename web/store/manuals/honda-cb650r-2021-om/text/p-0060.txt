# Helmet Holder and Tool Kit
The helmet holder is located underside of the
front seat. The tool kit and helmet set wire (in
the tool kit) are located under the rear seat.
u Use the helmet holder only when parked.
# Removing the Rear Seat (P.82)
3WARNING
Riding with a helmet attached to the
holder can interfere with your ability to
safely operate the vehicle and could
lead to a crash in which you can be
seriously hurt or killed.
Use the helmet holder only while
parked. Do not ride with a helmet
secured by the holder.
