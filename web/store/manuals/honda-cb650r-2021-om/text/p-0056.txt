Starting the Engine
Start your engine using the following
procedure, regardless of whether the engine
is cold or warm.
NOTICE
• If the engine does not start within 5 seconds, turn the
ignition switch to the OFF position and wait 10 seconds
before trying to start the engine again to recover
battery voltage.
• Extended fast idling and revving the engine can
damage the engine, and the exhaust system.
• Snapping the throttle or fast idling for more than about
5 minutes may cause exhaust pipe discoloration.
• The engine will not start if the throttle is fully open.
aMake sure the engine stop switch is in the 
(Run) position.
bTurn the ignition switch to the ON position.
cShift the transmission to Neutral ( N indicator
comes on). Alternatively, pull in the clutch lever
to start your vehicle with the transmission in
gear so long as the side stand is raised.
dPress the start button with the throttle
completely closed.
u If you cannot start the engine, open the
throttle slightly (about 0.1 in (3 mm), without
freeplay) and press the start button.
