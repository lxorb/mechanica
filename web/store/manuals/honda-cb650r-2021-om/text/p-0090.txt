Inspecting the Brake Pads
Check the condition of the brake pad wear
indicators.
Front The pads need to be replaced if a
brake pad is worn to the bottom of the
indicator.
Rear The pads need to be replaced if a brake
pad is worn to the indicator.
1.
Front Inspect the brake pads from in front
of the brake caliper.
u Always inspect both left and right
brake calipers.
2.
Rear Inspect the brake pads from the rear
right of the vehicle.
If necessary have the pads replaced by your
dealer.
Always replace both left and right brake pads
at the same time.
