04 · Grip

Built for a gloved thumb. A deep-green ground, cream tiles that sit on a 4 px solid edge and drop onto it when pressed, lime for what you touch and tangerine for what the manual says. Targets are 54–62 px, corners 18–26 px, and the motion overshoots on purpose. Different because it is the only direction with a coloured ground and a physical press — the app feels like a tool, not a document viewer.

3D step

The render keeps its own cream ground, so the viewer is simply the biggest tile on the screen — rounded 26 px, sitting on the same 4 px edge as everything else. Explode is a spring: the groups fly apart with a small overshoot and settle. The chosen part gets a 4 px lime ring, and a lime pill carrying the part name and its page detaches from the ring and travels down into the list, landing on the matching row. That travel is the only animation that crosses two regions, and it is what makes the list feel caused by the model.

Marker

A fat tangerine swipe at 50% multiply with 8 px rounded ends that overshoot the text by 6 px, drawn in 300 ms, 70 ms apart — a real highlighter that does not stop neatly at the last letter. The page pill at the top turns lime at the same moment, so the colour of "you are here" and the colour of "this is the bit" stay different.

Risk

Friendly can read as unserious next to an official document, and two accents plus a coloured ground is the most colour any of the five spends. Rounded cream tiles are also the house style of every consumer app of the last five years.
