01 · Torque

A pit-wall instrument: carbon ground, one amber needle colour, a redline that only ever means "last page". Every number in the app — year, chapter, page — is set as an instrument readout in tabular Saira, so the manual's own numbering reads like timing data. Different because it is the only direction where the chrome is an instrument and the page is the readout it points at.

3D step

The model hangs on a dark stage under a single amber sweep arc. Picking a section drives the explode: parts travel outward along the arc over 420 ms and the matched group snaps to amber while everything else drops to 40% — the same move the rev needle makes, reused. The part name and its page sit in one amber tag welded to the stage floor, read as a pit-board line: FRONT BRAKE · 84.

Marker

Amber at 42% multiply over the grayscale page, stroked left→right in 260 ms with a 60 ms stagger per line, like a bar filling. The final marked line carries a 2 px redline tick at its left edge: the limit, the last thing you need to read.

Risk

Carbon + amber is the default costume of every bike app on the store, and an instrument is a live gauge while a manual page is static. It survives only because the numbers it instruments are real.
