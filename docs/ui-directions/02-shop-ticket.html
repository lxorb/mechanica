02 · Shop Ticket

The app is the paperwork. Warm stock, dashed rules, punched black label strips, part numbers in typewriter mono, a blueprint grid under the exploded fiche. Everything on screen is printed matter — Different because it is the only direction where the interface and the document are made of the same material, so the PDF page never looks pasted in.

3D step

The model is a parts fiche: the exploded render printed in grey multiply straight onto a 14 px blueprint grid, no panel, no frame. Explode pulls the groups apart along their leader lines over 300 ms. The chosen part is not tinted — it is ringed in red grease pencil with a leader running to a black label strip: FRONT BRAKE · p.84. One circle, one line, the way a parts catalogue calls out item ❶.

Marker

Wax-pencil yellow at 55% multiply, rotated −0.35° so it never lines up perfectly with the type, drawn L→R in 240 ms. Every marked band carries a 3 px red bar in the margin, and the relevant pages stick out of the sheet as red index tabs on the right edge — the marker you can see with the phone in your pocket.

Risk

Warm paper + mono is close to what is already shipped, so it reads as a refinement rather than a change of mind; and a light UI in a garage at night is the wrong brightness.
