What of Deepgram, and why

why
Voice Agent API, not STT + LLM + TTS stitchedOne socket carries mic up, voice down and the JSON events interleaved — so the turn boundary, the barge-in signal and the function call are decided next to the audio. And the tool calls are server-side, which is what makes the grounding airtight.
flux-general-en endpointingEnd of turn is detected inside the model, not by a silence timer. Eager EOT starts the LLM before the sentence ends and throws the speculative turn away on TurnResumed. 1.35 s to first audio against nova-3's 1.97 s.
Barge-inA workshop answer is often wrong-question-shaped. Scheduled audio dies on the next render quantum, <3 ms.
Keyterm prompting — 100 terms, built per manualAccepted live with zero errors. We could not measure a win: 11.4% vs 13.6% WER at 3 dB, 14.1% vs 13.3% at 0 dB. That is noise, not a result. It ships because it is the documented lever, it costs nothing, and it protects the proper nouns.

The claim we are entitled to make is "built and wired", not "X% better". A real number needs real mechanics in a real shop — and that is the first thing we would spend a Deepgram credit on.
